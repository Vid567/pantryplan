Questions before you start

Is PantryPlan free?

Yes. The current Browser Beta is free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start using PantryPlan without creating an account.

Where is my pantry data stored?

Your pantry data stays on your device rather than being stored in a PantryPlan cloud account.

Can I use a shopping list?

Yes. PantryPlan includes a shopping workflow alongside your stock overview.

Can I add products with my voice?

Voice input is available where your browser and device support speech recognition. You can also add and edit products manually.
