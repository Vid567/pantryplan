# PantryPlan — Read Me First

## What is PantryPlan?
PantryPlan keeps track of what you have at home and what you need to buy — hands-free. Say what you need out loud and everything lands on your list. In the store, one tap moves each item from your list into your pantry.

## What you need
- A phone with Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone)
- The app hosted on a web address (https) — voice does NOT work when the file is opened directly from phone storage
- Nothing else: no account, no installation, no internet needed after first load

## Files in this folder
- pantryplan-app.html   — the app itself
- PantryPlan-GUIDE.html — visual guide (upload alongside the app, same folder)
- PantryPlan-READ-ME-FIRST.txt — this file
- PantryPlan-Voice-Activation-Changes.txt — full technical changelog

## Deploying to Netlify (one-time)
1. Log in to app.netlify.com → your site → Deploys tab
2. Drag BOTH files onto the deploy drop zone at once:
   - pantryplan-app.html
   - PantryPlan-GUIDE.html
3. Done — same URL, updated instantly

Do NOT rename pantryplan-app.html to index.html — the guide iframes reference
it by name. If your Netlify site also hosts DailyCashPlan as index.html, they
will coexist without conflict.

## Your data & privacy
- Everything is stored on your own phone — nothing is sent anywhere
- Voice is processed by your phone's built-in speech recognition
- Backup: More → Export data. Restore on any device with Import backup
- Clearing browser data erases the app's data — export first

## Language
English only — speak English regardless of your phone's language setting.
(A Dutch version is planned as a separate app.)

## Known limitations
- Speech recognition sometimes mishears (say "chicken eggs" not just "eggs")
- Pause briefly between items so each is heard clearly
- Mic stops automatically after 10 seconds of silence
- Firefox desktop does not support voice

## Browser support
- Chrome (Android): full support
- Safari (iPhone): full support
- Firefox desktop: voice not supported (type-only fallback shown)
