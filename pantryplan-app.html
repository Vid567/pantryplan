Move safely to a new phone

  1. On your old phone, tap Export data.
  2. iPhone/iPad: save the JSON backup in Files or your personal iCloud Drive.
    Android: find it in Downloads, or save it to your personal Google Drive.
  3. Open PantryPlan on the new phone, go to More, then tap Import backup and select the file.
  4. Check that your pantry, shopping list and photos are present before erasing anything on the old phone.

The same backup works between iPhone and Android.

Keep it private: the backup can contain your item photos. Store it in your own account and avoid sharing it publicly.
