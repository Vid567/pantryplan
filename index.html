Quick start

How to get started

Open the app on Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). Voice won't work on Firefox or when opening the file from phone storage.

1

Open the app on your phone

Tap Open PantryPlan above. Add it to your home screen via your browser menu for the full app experience.

2

Allow microphone access

The first time you tap the mic, your phone will ask for permission. Tap Allow. Without this, voice won't work.

3

Say what you have or need

Speak naturally: "milk, chicken eggs and 2 bags of rice". Pause briefly between items. The mic stops by itself after 10 seconds of silence.

4

Take it to the store

Switch to the 🛒 Shopping tab. Tap 🗑 when each item goes in your cart — it leaves the list and lands in your pantry automatically.
