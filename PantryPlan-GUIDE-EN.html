The three main areas

🥫 Pantry

See what you have at home, add products, change quantities and mark when stock is running low.

🛒 Shopping list

Keep track of what you need to buy. Check products while shopping and move bought items back to the pantry when you are done.

⚙️ Data & backup

Manage local data, export/import and Beta options. PantryPlan keeps your pantry, shopping list and photos on your device.

Using voice

The English version uses English speech recognition (en-US).

For best recognition: speak clearly, keep the phone close and pause briefly between products. Recording stops automatically after about 10 seconds of silence.
What you want to doExample
Add products“rice, watermelon and two cartons of milk”
Update pantry“out of eggs”
Remove a product“remove pasta”
Add shopping items“bananas, three yogurt and two bags of rice”

Always check what PantryPlan added or changed after voice input. Speech recognition can misunderstand a word.

Adding products to your pantry

Use Add item or the microphone. Tap a product name to open its details. There you can change the name, quantity, category and photo.

Using the shopping list

Add products manually or with voice. Check items while shopping. When finished, move bought products back to the pantry.

Fixing mistakes and editing products

Tap a product to open the entry screen. Change the details and choose Save. After voice input you can undo the latest change if recognition was incorrect.

Photos

Open a product and use the photo option to take or choose a photo. Photos remain part of the local PantryPlan data on your device.

Data and backup

Use export to create a backup of your PantryPlan data. Import lets you restore a previous backup.
