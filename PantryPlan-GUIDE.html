⚙️ More — backup and phone transfer

Export your pantry as a JSON backup file. On iPhone or iPad, save it in Files or your personal iCloud Drive. On Android, use Downloads or your personal Google Drive. Open PantryPlan on the new phone, choose More → Import backup, and select the file. The same backup works between iPhone and Android.

Check that the pantry, shopping list and photos arrived before erasing the old phone. Backups can contain item photos, so keep the file in your own account and do not share it publicly.

Tap the blue mic button. Speak naturally. Say as many items as you like. The mic stops by itself after 10 seconds of silence.

Edit screen

Change the name, quantity, or category. Tap Save to confirm. Tap 🗑 Delete this item (red) to remove it permanently. Tap ✕ or Cancel to go back without changes.

What you can do here

1
Fix a wrong name

If voice added "Hotmail", tap the item, change the name to "Oatmeal", tap Save.

2
Change category

Tap any chip to re-file the item. Auto-categorisation sometimes guesses wrong — this is the override.

3
Add / change photo

Tap 📷 Take / choose photo. The photo then shows on the main Pantry screen.

4
Delete the item

Scroll to the bottom and tap the red 🗑 Delete this item button.
